

Araucaria Forest:
    * witches (undead) hides in the fog, and throws fog bottles (may be cured)
    * Structures: (related to the witches)
    * abandoned pillager outposts (maybe skeleton pillager)
    * Fog: volumetric fog caused by potions (thrown and in cauldron)
    * potion of true sight: lets you see invisible entities, and through fog for a few seconds (brewed from araucaria needles

Ginkgo Forest:
    * increased pig spawns
    * Ginkgo tree: big and small
    * ruins
    * potential graveyard structure
    * decently lush/bright colors
    * ginkgo ground litter

Ghost Pipe:
    * https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UVJ6CmwtFi4&list=PLKGarocXCE1H9Y21-pxjt5Pt8bW14twa-&index=40
    * generate in dark oak (and pale garden) and structures in ginkgo (maybe potted in araucaria witch structure)
    * can be crafted into black dye
    * damaged texture
    * Anti Growing Area
    * potion effect brewed from dried ghost pipe (dried on a campfire), disables regaining health for a few moments
    * spreads when bonemealed

Butterfly Weed:
    * generate in Flower forest, cherry grove, ginkgo, and rarely in plans and sunflower plains
    * can be crafted into orange dye
    * Custom Data Driven Butterfly Paricles
    * butterfly types being determined by surrounding blocks (any butterfly if no specific blocks)
    * drops a duplicate when bonemealed
